Publish yours

Put it in a public repository. That’s it.

There’s nothing to upload. Put your system in a public repository, under systems/<name>/ or at its root, add the repository topic fnloom-system, and the marketplace finds it, builds it, checks it and lists it at that exact version. Push a new tag to publish an update.

Don’t want to write code? Ask fnloom to build the system with you; it writes the folder, checks it and installs it on your own hub first.

your-repo/
└─ systems/
   └─ uptime-checks/
      ├─ system.json      name, version, what it does
      ├─ server/main.ts   its fns
      ├─ client/          optional: its own screens
      └─ marketplace/     optional: icon, screenshots
